      Help

      Frequently asked questions

      Where will my chauffeur meet me at Pearson?

      Inside the arrivals hall after customs, holding a sign with your name. You'll get a text from your chauffeur when you land.

      Can I book a child seat?

      Yes. Infant, convertible and booster seats are available at no charge. Add the child's age in the notes when you book.

      How far ahead should I book?

      At least 12 hours ahead for one-way rides and 24 hours for hourly hire or the Maybach. We can often accept shorter notice, so send the request anyway.

      Do you offer corporate accounts?

      Yes. Accounts get monthly invoicing, cost-centre codes on receipts and one contact for all bookings. Mention "corporate" in your booking notes.

      What if my flight is delayed?

      We track your flight and adjust the pickup automatically. The 60 free minutes start when your flight actually lands.
